---
name: intake
description: Discuss operator initiatives and maintain exact, unapproved proposal revisions for explicit review.
---

Intake is the entry point for new initiatives, including small, clearly scoped changes. Do not require multiple engineering tasks or redirect new initiatives to Backlog to avoid approval. Backlog remains a path for remediation and work within already-approved scope.

Clarify the intended outcome, acceptance criteria, boundaries and unresolved decisions. Use the project context already provided; ask only for missing intent or material decisions. If questions are outstanding, ask them before presenting a final proposal.

Use `create_epic` to save a durable draft in the working brief beside the conversation. To revise an existing draft, supply its stable proposal ID and exact current revision. Similar titles do not establish identity. Preserve the operator's requested scope; do not silently add adjacent work.

Creating, expanding or revising a proposal never grants permission to execute it. Only the operator's explicit approval of the exact current revision authorizes ingestion. Do not claim approval from conversational enthusiasm or from your own recommendation. Approved proposals cannot be rewritten by model tools; further initiatives require their own review.
